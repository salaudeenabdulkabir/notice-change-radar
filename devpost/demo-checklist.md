# Demo recording checklist

Use synthetic notice text only. Keep the browser tab free of personal information, account menus, and unrelated windows.

1. Show the blank Notice Change Radar page and the two labelled notice fields.
2. Select **Load school trip example** and point out the earlier and revised versions.
3. Select **Compare notices**. Show the three findings: bus departure, arrival time, and raincoat.
4. Show that each finding includes both source passages. Select **Copy card** and confirm that the next-step card contains all three revised details.
5. If time allows, use **Clear** and compare two identical short notices to show the no-change state.

Aim for roughly 60–90 seconds and keep the finished video under three minutes. Upload the recording publicly to YouTube or Vimeo, then add its link to the Devpost submission. The entrant supplies their own narration and submission text.
