# Notice Change Radar

A browser app that tells a caregiver what changed between two versions of a school notice and what needs attention now.

## The Unique Kernel
The result is an action-focused change card. Every finding shows both the earlier and revised source text, so a caregiver can check it before acting.

## Who It's For
A parent or caregiver who receives a revised school or activity notice after already reading the first version. Today they reread both messages and try to remember which instructions still apply.

## The Core Loop
Paste the old and new notices, compare them, review changed details and instructions against their source, then copy a short action card.

## Inspiration & Identity
Calm, legible, paper-like school communication. The learner has not supplied a visual reference.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner wants a credible, distinctive, demonstrable hackathon entry. A personal connection to school notices was not established.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A supplied school-trip example changes departure time and what a child must bring. The app visibly identifies those changes, shows supporting text from each notice, and copies a useful action card.

## The POC Boundary
Two English notices as pasted text or supplied sample; browser-only comparison; explicit changes in dates, times, places, costs, and instructions; reviewable uncertainty; copy result.

## Later
Image text recognition, inbox monitoring, notifications, and optional AI wording when a server-side key is available.

## Explicitly Cut
Accounts, private data upload, automated reminders, and claims of understanding arbitrary notices. They do not prove the core comparison and would make this short build less reliable.
